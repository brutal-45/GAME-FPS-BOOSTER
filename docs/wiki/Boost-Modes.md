# 🎯 Boost Modes

BRUTAL-FPS ships **6 boost modes**, each a complete tuning profile. In the app,
open **⚡ BRUTAL BOOST** and click a mode, then press **💀 BRUTAL BOOST 💀**.

| Mode | Icon | Typical gain | Best for |
|------|------|--------------|----------|
| 🟢 **Balanced** | 🟢 | +30% | Safe, everyday gaming on any PC |
| ⚡ **Performance** | ⚡ | +40% | Competitive gamers, standard hardware |
| 🔥 **Brutal** | 🔥 | +50% | Serious FPS gains, good cooling |
| 💀 **Extreme** | 💀 | +60% | Maximum performance, strong cooling |
| 🥔 **Potato** | 🥔 | +45% | Low-end PCs — max resource savings |
| 🌙 **Silent** | 🌙 | +25% | Laptops — quiet fans, still fast |

---

## What a boost applies

When you hit **BRUTAL BOOST**, the app applies its full tuning profile:

- ⚡ **High Performance power plan** (max CPU/GPU clocks)
- 🎮 **Game Mode** — prioritise game processes, suspend background tasks
- 🧹 **Background cleanup** — free RAM by closing non-essential processes
- 📱 **Emulator priority boost** — high priority for Android emulators
- 🌐 **Network tuning** — lower latency for online games
- 🖥️ **Process priorities** — your game gets High/Real-time priority

## Tips

- **Start with Balanced** — it's the safest profile and works everywhere.
- **Use Potato** on weak machines; it trades visuals/system extras for playable FPS.
- **Use Silent** on battery-powered laptops.
- **Extreme** is best on desktops with good cooling; watch temps in
  📊 Dashboard after applying.
- All profiles are **anti-cheat safe** — they tune the OS, not the game.
