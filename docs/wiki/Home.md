# 💀 BRUTAL-FPS — Wiki

Welcome to the **BRUTAL-FPS** wiki — the ultimate gaming performance booster.

**BRUTAL-FPS** is a 100% free, open-source gaming performance booster that applies
one-click tuning profiles (power plans, process priorities, latency and background
tweaks) to give you **30–60% more FPS** in your favourite games.
