# ❓ FAQ

## 💰 Is BRUTAL-FPS really free?

**Yes — 100% free, forever.** No premium version, no paywalls, no ads, no hidden
costs. MIT-licensed, open source.

## 🛡️ Is it anti-cheat safe?

Yes. BRUTAL-FPS tunes **operating-system settings** (power plans, process priority,
background tasks, network latency) — it does not inject into game processes or
modify game files. It is compatible with Vanguard, EAC, BattlEye, VAC and friends.

> ⚠️ As with any performance tool, use it with normal caution and check the rules
> of any competitive game/platform you play.

## 📈 How much FPS will I gain?

Typically **+30% to +60%**, depending on your hardware and the mode:
Balanced ≈ +30%, Performance ≈ +40%, Brutal ≈ +50%, Extreme ≈ +60%,
Potato ≈ +45%, Silent ≈ +25%. The biggest gains come on low-end systems and
through emulators.

## 💻 Which platforms are supported?

- **Windows** 7/8/10/11
- **macOS** 10.13+ (Intel and Apple Silicon)
- **Linux** — Debian, Ubuntu, Linux Mint, Pop!_OS, **Kali**, Fedora/Arch (AppImage)

## 📱 Does it work with emulators?

Yes — the app can optimize **BlueStacks, LDPlayer, Nox Player, MEmu, GameLoop,
SmartGaGa** with one click (Emulators tab).

## 🎮 Can I add my own game?

Yes. Open **🎮 Games** → **＋ Add Game**, type the name, then **🎯 Optimize**.

## 🔄 Will it reset my system settings?

By default BRUTAL-FPS can **auto-restore** original settings when you stop a boost
(Settings → General → **Auto restore**). Keep this on if you want the tool to revert
changes automatically.

## 🧠 Does it save my settings?

Yes — preferences, tweaks, optimized games/emulators and achievements are stored
locally on your machine.

## 📦 Where do I download it?

The [Releases page](https://github.com/brutal-45/GAME-FPS-BOOSTER/releases) —
installers for Windows, macOS and Linux (including the Kali package).

## 🧰 Is there a browser/standalone version?

Yes — `public/brutal-fps-standalone.html` is a self-contained version of the full
dashboard and is what the desktop apps load too.
